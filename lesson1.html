📘 Lesson 1: What is Building Technology?

Introduction to the Trade

📖 Definition

Building Technology is the study and practical application of knowledge, skills, tools, materials and methods used in the construction of buildings and other structures.

Remember: Building Technology combines knowledge and practical skills to construct safe, functional, durable and attractive structures.

🏗️ What does Building Technology involve?

  • Reading and understanding building drawings.
  • Using building tools and equipment.
  • Measuring and calculating materials.
  • Preparing and using building materials.
  • Constructing walls and foundations.
  • Following health and safety procedures.
  • Finishing and maintaining buildings.

🎯 Importance of Building Technology

Building Technology helps builders understand how buildings are designed, constructed and maintained correctly.

It helps to ensure that structures are safe, strong, durable and suitable for their intended purpose.

🧠 Learning Check

Before moving to the next lesson, make sure you can explain:

  • What Building Technology means.

  • Why Building Technology is important.
